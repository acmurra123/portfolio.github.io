About Annette C. Murray

Annette C. Murray is a filmmaker, cinematographer, and photographer based in Ithaca and New York City. Through both moving and still images, she seeks to capture the beauty and complexity of the human experience.

As a cinematographer, Annette enjoys exploring distinct visual styles and collaborating with directors to bring their visions to life. She is particularly drawn to stories exploring the female experience and interpersonal relationships across both fiction and nonfiction.

Her passion for visual storytelling extends and into photography. Working with both 35mm film and digital, she approaches photography with an appreciation for composition, light, and the details of everyday life, capturing the world as she sees it. Her photographic work spans weddings, graduations, portraits, editorial work, and behind-the-scenes documentation of film productions.

Annette is currently an undergraduate at Cornell University studying Film and Psychology.
