About Annette C. Murray

Annette C. Murray is a biracial filmmaker, cinematographer, and photographer from Queens, New York. Growing up in one of the most diverse places in the world shaped her appreciation for the beauty and intricacies of everyday life. Through her work, she strives to tell stories that are beautiful in both style and meaning, so that those who watch may feel seen.

As a cinematographer, Annette enjoys exploring distinct visual styles and collaborating with directors to bring their visions to life. She is particularly drawn to stories exploring the female experience, interpersonal relationships, and the complexities of human behavior, across both fiction and nonfiction.

Her love for creating beautiful images extends beyond the screen and into photography. Shooting on both 35mm film and digital, she finds inspiration in candid moments, fleeting interactions, and the often-overlooked beauty of everyday life.

Annette is currently an undergraduate at Cornell University studying Film and Psychology.
